How AFM Concierge Works

Large firms bring thousands of employees together for an annual meeting, often across several cities at once. AFM Concierge gives every attendee a personal AI assistant for the event.

The Problem

Before a big company meeting, the events team's inbox fills up with the same questions: What time does it start in my city? Where is the venue? Is there a livestream? What should I wear? Answering each one by hand takes hours, and attendees in other time zones often wait a full day for a reply.

When an event happens in several cities at the same time, the problem multiplies. Every location has its own venue, schedule, and travel details, and one wrong answer can send someone to the wrong place.

How It Works

  1. Load the event info. The events team uploads agendas, venue details, FAQs, and travel guides for each city.
  2. Attendees pick their city. The chatbot first asks where you are attending: Dallas, Toronto, London, Hyderabad, or the livestream.
  3. AI answers instantly. The assistant uses only the approved event information to answer questions, so responses stay accurate and on-brand.
  4. Hand-off when needed. If a question needs a human, the bot points the attendee to the right events team contact.

Who It's For

Corporate events teams running large internal meetings, conferences, or kickoffs. Attendees who want quick answers without digging through emails. Leadership who want a smooth, consistent experience for every location.
