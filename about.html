How GiftSpark Works

We built GiftSpark because finding the right gift is stressful. Most people spend hours scrolling, then settle for a gift card. Our AI does the hard part for you.

The Problem

Americans spend billions of dollars every year on gifts that end up returned, re-gifted, or forgotten in a closet. The problem isn't a lack of options. It's that there are too many. Online stores show millions of products, but none of them know the person you're shopping for.

GiftSpark fixes this by acting like a thoughtful friend who knows a little about everything. You tell it about the person, and it narrows millions of products down to a short, personal list.

How It Works

  1. Describe them. Share the person's age, relationship to you, hobbies, and anything special about them, in your own words.
  2. Set the details. Choose a budget and an occasion, like a birthday, anniversary, holiday, or a "just because" surprise.
  3. AI does the matching. Our generative AI model reads your description and compares it with thousands of gift ideas, trends, and reviews to find the best fit.
  4. Pick and shop. You get 5–10 personalized ideas, each with a short reason why it fits, and links to buy from trusted stores.

Who It's For

Busy professionals who want to give thoughtful gifts but don't have time to research. Students shopping on a budget. Companies sending client and employee gifts at scale, where GiftSpark Business can tailor gifts for hundreds of people at once.
